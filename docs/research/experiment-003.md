# Experiment 003 · Mathematical Weight

**Status** Historical comparison with supplied single-listener ratings.

## Objective and supplied parameters

Explore how perceived musical qualities vary with mathematical weight.

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Generator | Golden Ratio |
| Reference frequency | 220 Hz |
| Tempo | 89 BPM |
| Scale | A Minor |
| Quantization | Enabled throughout |

## Recorded results

The criteria and 1–10 scale match [Experiment 001](experiment-001.md).

| Mathematical weight | Consonance | Emotionality | Melodicity | Originality | Willingness to listen again | Total / 50 |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0% | 10 | 1 | 10 | 1 | 5 | 27 |
| 10% | 10 | 9 | 10 | 10 | 7 | 46 |
| 25% | 10 | 9 | 10 | 10 | 6 | 45 |
| 50% | 7 | 7 | 7 | 8 | 6 | 35 |
| 75% | 10 | 10 | 10 | 8 | 8 | 46 |
| 100% | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 8 | 40 |

## Observations

- The observed totals were nonmonotonic. The highest measured totals were 46/50 at 10%
  and 75%, each 19 points above the 0% control.
- At 75%, emotionality and willingness to listen again were each one point higher than
  at 10%. Originality was two points higher at 10% than at 75%.
- Quantization stayed enabled at 100%. Mathematical weight is a blend parameter.
  The percentage of notes changed is a separate measure.
- Quantization can map sequences generated at different weights to the same output.
  Duplicate output across these particular stimuli has not been verified.

## Limitations and follow-up

The ratings came from one listener using one generator and one source sequence. The
evaluation was subjective and lacked a blind protocol. Verified original audio is
unavailable in the repository. The results motivate [H-001 and H-002](hypotheses.md)
and the planned [fine sweep](experiment-004.md).
