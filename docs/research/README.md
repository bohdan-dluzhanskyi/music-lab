# Music Lab research

## Objective

Investigate how mathematical and geometric patterns can help generate music and complement
classical music theory.

## Stage 14 · Future Experiments Laboratory

The [roadmap](../roadmap.md) places the following capabilities in the planned laboratory.
These inputs are for future specification and planning.

- **Study design** — parameter sweeps, blind listening, randomized presentation,
  and subjective evaluation.
- **Reproducibility** — experiment history, reproducible generation, and algorithm
  versioning. Follow the approved [generation provenance policy](../decisions/adr-005-generation-provenance.md).
- **Comparison and measurement** — score heatmaps, sequence similarity, duplicate detection,
  interval analysis, quantization analysis, note-level difference analysis, and objective metrics.
- **Research output** — export research data with enough context to interpret or repeat a run.
