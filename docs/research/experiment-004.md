# Experiment 004 · Fine Weight Sweep

**Status** Planned. Results pending.

## Objective

Test whether the high totals observed at 10% and 75% in
[Experiment 003](experiment-003.md) persist under a finer sweep and controlled comparisons.

## Planned conditions

Sweep 5%, 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 60%, 65%, 70%, 75%, 80%, and 85%.

- Use blind listening and randomized presentation order.
- Compare quantization enabled and disabled.
- Explore different scales and reference melodies.
- Detect duplicate sequences across conditions.
- Analyze differences at the note level and compare interval distributions.

The exact stimuli, measurement rules, and listening protocol remain to be defined
using the [experiment methodology](../experiments.md). Relevant hypotheses are
[H-001, H-002, and H-003](hypotheses.md).
