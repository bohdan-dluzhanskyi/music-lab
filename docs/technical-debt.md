# Technical debt register

These entries record the audit baseline. High priority marks blockers for the adjacent notation
work. Medium priority marks correctness or scalability work requiring a defined scope. Low
priority marks maintainability work. Remedies and exit checks are proposals for the linked
[roadmap](roadmap.md) stages.

| ID and priority | Problem, cause, and affected modules | Impact | Proposed resolution and stage | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TD-001 · High | [Note Inspector](../src/features/piano-roll/NoteInspector.tsx) and [Composition Core](../src/core/composition/composition.ts) accept values that the exact decomposition in [Notation Core](../src/core/notation/rhythm.ts) cannot represent. | Both staff and tab preparation can fail on valid events or the gaps they create. The audit reproduced failures for start/duration `0.1 / 0.5` and `0 / 0.3` beats. | 11D. Apply [ADR-001](decisions/adr-001-exact-musical-data-preservation.md) using a deterministic derived rhythm with visible deviation, respecting bar boundaries, order, voices, and rests. | Both views render those cases and a previously saved project. The supported `0.125 / 0.125` case stays exact. An approximation reports its error, and unrelated notes and canonical audio timing are preserved. |
| TD-002 · High | [Transport](../src/components/Transport/Transport.tsx) selects sequence mode only for Composer and Piano Roll. On Notation it uses lab Play, BPM, loop, and readout. Stop still targets Sequencer. | Users cannot control score playback consistently from Notation. | 11D. Define one composition transport contract and a cursor driven by Sequencer position. | Composer, Piano Roll, and Notation agree on Play, Pause, Stop, Seek, Loop, BPM, and position. Lab preview retains its own controls. |
| TD-003 · Medium | [NotationEngine](../src/engines/notation/NotationEngine.ts) draws ties only when adjacent fragments share a row. The core already marks continuations. | Sustained notes lose their visible connection at system breaks. | 11D. Render outgoing and incoming ties at row boundaries for supported voices and chords. | A browser case shows continuity across a row break and retains proper same-row ligatures and source IDs. |
| TD-004 · Medium | [Sequencer](../src/engines/audio/Sequencer.ts) uses a snapshot. Parts of the UI block edits, while [composition state](../src/stores/composition.store.ts) permits mutations. | Current score and scheduled audio can diverge. Seek retriggers the remainder of an already active note, which needs a documented playback contract. | 11D. Apply [ADR-002](decisions/adr-002-playback-editing-policy.md) and [ADR-003](decisions/adr-003-seek-behaviour.md). | Verify edit locking during initialization and playback, allowed controls, restored editing, and fresh composition on resume. Repeated seek uses the source frequency and remaining duration, honors track mix, and produces no duplicate scheduling. |
| TD-005 · Medium | [Mathematics Lab](../src/pages/MathematicsPage/MathematicsPage.tsx) uses project BPM. Transfer into [Composer](../src/stores/composition.store.ts) preserves beats and notes but omits lab BPM and the full generator provenance. | A transfer can sound at a different tempo. A generated track alone cannot reproduce the experiment. | 12 and 14. Apply [ADR-004](decisions/adr-004-mathematics-transfer-tempo.md) and [ADR-005](decisions/adr-005-generation-provenance.md). | Keep composition BPM by default and change it only by explicit choice. Verify reproducible origin records and their preservation after editing and reloading. |
| TD-006 · Medium | [Composition persistence](../src/stores/composition.store.ts) has a version and no explicit validation or next-version migration. [Pitch](../src/core/types/music.types.ts) stores derived values alongside source frequency. | Malformed or old data can violate timing, reference, and pitch invariants. | 12. Validate project boundaries, reconstruct derived pitch, provide migration and recovery. | Fixtures cover supported old versions, invalid values, and contradictory pitch fields. Recovery preserves valid data and reports failures. |
| TD-007 · Medium | The [accepted composition frequencies](../src/core/composition/composition.ts) extend beyond the displayed pitch rows in [Piano Roll](../src/features/piano-roll/PianoRoll.tsx). The endpoints are computed from a bounded MIDI viewport. | Edge pitches can be hidden or clipped. | 11F. Specify visibility or diagnostics for supported pitches without altering frequency. | Tests cover low and high valid pitches and make their events accessible. |
| TD-008 · Medium | [TypeScript configuration](../tsconfig.app.json) omits strict mode. | Some type contracts, particularly nullable values, have weaker compile-time enforcement. | 17. Inventory strictness errors and adopt strict checks with targeted fixes. | Strict TypeScript verification passes without broad suppressions. |
| TD-009 · Medium | [Sequencer](../src/engines/audio/Sequencer.ts) schedules the whole playback range at startup. [Score preparation](../src/core/notation/score.ts) scans voice groups for every measure. [Piano Roll](../src/features/piano-roll/PianoRoll.tsx) materializes the whole SVG grid and subscribes to playback position. | Long works can incur high startup and render costs. The audit did not benchmark these workloads. | 11D and 17. Isolate cursor updates, measure representative projects, and target the measured costs. | An agreed workload meets measured startup and interaction budgets. Cursor movement does not rebuild score layout. |
| TD-010 · Medium | [Router](../src/app/router.tsx) imports pages eagerly. The audited production build emitted a 2,036.50 kB minified entry script, 962.89 kB gzip, and a large-chunk warning. | Higher startup download and parse costs are plausible. Per-library costs were not measured. | 17. Profile bundle composition and evaluate route or engine boundaries for splitting. | Report before and after initial and route-specific sizes against an agreed budget, with startup and offline behavior verified. |
| TD-011 · Low | [Theory](../src/core/music/quantization.ts) and [Composition](../src/core/composition/quantization.ts) have overlapping quantization and blend rules. [Fingering](../src/core/notation/fingering.ts) and [chord fingering](../src/core/notation/chordFingering.ts) have different capabilities. | Changes can drift, while blind consolidation can change pitch range, reference tuning, or fret choices. | 17. Inventory callers and invariants, then share equivalent domain rules. Review the declared but currently unused Tonal dependency as part of the inventory. | Tests cover current contract differences. Equivalent calculations have one implementation and dependency decisions are recorded. |
| TD-012 · Low | The audit found a default Vite README and a tracked `music-lab-source.zip` with undocumented purpose. | Onboarding and provenance of the archive were unclear. | Resolved by this documentation change. The [README](../README.md) documents the project. [ADR-006](decisions/adr-006-repository-hygiene.md) removes the snapshot from Git and retains the ignored local copy. | Verify that Git omits the archive, the root-scoped rule matches it, and the local file is intact. |
| TD-013 · Medium | [Unit suites](../src/tests) cover domain logic and audio constants, while VexFlow SVG, Web Audio, and cross-view behavior lacked direct evidence in the audit. | A passing unit suite cannot detect score layout, audible behavior, or synchronization regressions. | 11D and 17. Add integration coverage and record browser and audio evidence for affected flows. | Acceptance runs exercise score rendering, position and selection, and audible play/stop on the specified environment. |

Analyzer, Experiments, Instruments, Mixer, Settings, exchange, and Tauri gaps are planned
capabilities in the roadmap. The debt register tracks defects and maintenance work within
or across those stages.
