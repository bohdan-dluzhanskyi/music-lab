# Technical debt register

These entries retain the audit baseline and record resolution where the original exit criteria have been met. High priority marks blockers for the adjacent notation
work. Medium priority marks correctness or scalability work requiring a defined scope. Low
priority marks maintainability work. Remedies and exit checks are proposals for the linked
[roadmap](roadmap.md) stages. See the [11D verification record](../specs/001-piano-grand-staff/verification.md) for browser, audio and performance evidence.

| ID and priority | Problem, cause, and affected modules | Impact | Proposed resolution and stage | Exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TD-001 · High | [Note Inspector](../src/features/piano-roll/NoteInspector.tsx) and [Composition Core](../src/core/composition/composition.ts) accept values that the exact decomposition in [Notation Core](../src/core/notation/rhythm.ts) cannot represent. | Both staff and tab preparation can fail on valid events or the gaps they create. The audit reproduced failures for start/duration `0.1 / 0.5` and `0 / 0.3` beats. | Closed in 11D. Staff and tab handle the original off-grid cases with visible deviations, preserve the exact reference case and retain the canonical source and schedule. | Both views render those cases and a previously saved project. The supported `0.125 / 0.125` case stays exact. An approximation reports its error, and unrelated notes and canonical audio timing are preserved. |
| TD-002 · High | [Transport](../src/components/Transport/Transport.tsx) selects sequence mode only for Composer and Piano Roll. On Notation it uses lab Play, BPM, loop, and readout. Stop still targets Sequencer. | Users cannot control score playback consistently from Notation. | Closed in 11D. Notation, Composer and Piano Roll share Transport and position. Browser and owner listening confirmed the relevant playback behavior. Lab preview remains separate. | Composer, Piano Roll, and Notation agree on Play, Pause, Stop, Seek, Loop, BPM, and position. Lab preview retains its own controls. |
| TD-003 · Medium | [NotationEngine](../src/engines/notation/NotationEngine.ts) draws ties only when adjacent fragments share a row. The core already marks continuations. | Sustained notes lose their visible connection at system breaks. | Closed in 11D. Browser and source-ID tests verified continuations within measures, across barlines and between systems. | A browser case shows continuity across a row break and retains proper same-row ligatures and source IDs. |
| TD-004 · Medium | [Sequencer](../src/engines/audio/Sequencer.ts) uses a snapshot. Parts of the UI block edits, while [composition state](../src/stores/composition.store.ts) permits mutations. | Current score and scheduled audio can diverge. Seek retriggers the remainder of an already active note, which needs a documented playback contract. | Closed in 11D. The store and UI enforce the edit gate. Browser and owner evidence confirmed Pause, resume, sustained-note seek and silence after Stop. | Verify edit locking during initialization and playback, allowed controls, restored editing, and fresh composition on resume. Repeated seek uses the source frequency and remaining duration, honors track mix, and produces no duplicate scheduling. |
| TD-005 · Medium | [Mathematics Lab](../src/pages/MathematicsPage/MathematicsPage.tsx) uses project BPM. | TD-008 · Medium | [TypeScript configuration](../tsconfig.app.json) omits strict mode. | Some type contracts, particularly nullable values, have weaker compile-time enforcement. | 17. | TD-009 · Medium | Full score SVG layout and replacement cost remain measurable on long scores. At 256 bars, the largest observed main-thread block was 222.2 ms. After 20 playback cycles, JS heap after GC rose by 16.4 MB. The cause is unknown and the observation does not establish a leak. The audio pool provisionally limits each track to 32 voices and deterministically skips and reports events when all voices are reserved. | Layout can interrupt interaction. Dense research scores can play fewer events than their canonical content. The source events remain intact. Observed Tone resources were released during the measured cycles. | Open. Reassess voice capacity in stage 15. Investigate heap retainers and optimize SVG by stage 17, or earlier if a material regression is confirmed. 11D established zero full layout redraws and zero redundant scrolls during the measured cursor-only windows. | Agree on latency, heap and voice budgets. Verify representative workloads, post-GC trends and dense-score playback against those budgets, and preserve cursor isolation. |
| TD-010 · Medium | [Router](../src/app/router.tsx) imports pages eagerly. The audited production build emitted a 2,036.50 kB minified entry script, 962.89 kB gzip, and a large-chunk warning. Profile bundle composition and evaluate route or engine boundaries for splitting. | Report before and after initial and route-specific sizes against an agreed budget, with startup and offline behavior verified. Confirm the intended local favicon behavior. |
